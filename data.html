Import from another service

Letterboxd export

Import watched films, ratings, diary facts, tags, and watchlist changes from your Letterboxd export ZIP into your account. Rankings and award ballots are never imported.

How do I get my Letterboxd export?

1. Open Letterboxd Settings → Data ↗
2. Click Export Your Data at the bottom of the page.
3. Upload the downloaded .zip file here.

The ZIP never leaves this browser.
Import from IMDb

IMDb export

Upload your IMDb ratings.csv and watchlist.csv exports to bring in watched films, star ratings, and your watchlist. 1–10 ratings are converted to the 0.5–5.0★ scale.

How do I get my IMDb export?

1. Open Your IMDb Ratings ↗
2. Click the three dots icon (···) or export link at the top right and choose Export.
3. (Optional) Open Your IMDb Watchlist ↗ and export your watchlist CSV.
4. Select your ratings.csv and/or watchlist.csv files here.

Import from Google Sheets

Google Sheets

Keep your archive in a Google Sheet. Create a formatted workbook on your Google Drive or connect an existing sheet, then sync changes in and push your archive back.

Google Drive integration

Instantiate a formatted workbook in your Google Drive with Watched, Watchlist, and Awards tabs.

Clicking this will open a Google sign-in prompt.

Sync reads the Watched tab, plus the Watchlist and Awards tabs when present. Tab names must match exactly.

Log films, ratings, and watchlist additions in your sheet.

Fetch updates and review additions before saving.

Push archive back

Review and push watched films, watchlist items, and Awards back to your connected workbook.

Which columns does each tab need?

A workbook made with Create on Google Drive already has these headers. Match them when connecting a sheet you set up yourself.

Watched Columns

Dynamic Rank, Rank, Year, Title, Director, Rating, Tag, Views, Date, Platform

A Rank of - marks a film you haven't ranked.

Watchlist Columns

Name, Year, Director, Tier, Tag

Awards Columns

Position and Period, then one or more columns per award category, such as Director (Recipient) and Director (Film).

Each period is a block of rows numbered 1 to 5 for a year, 10 for a decade, 20 for a century, and 25 for all-time. The block's first Period cell names the type (Year, Decade, Century or All-time) and the second names the period, such as 1999 or 1990s. Create on Google Drive lays out every block from 1900 to 2029, the 1900s to 2020s, the 20th and 21st centuries, and all-time. Push adds any missing periods. Blank blocks leave your awards unchanged when syncing; a populated block replaces its supplied award categories. Leave a dash (-) in an award film cell to clear a whole ballot.
