Import from another service

Letterboxd export

Preview the original Letterboxd ZIP, then save watched films, ratings, diary facts, tags, and watchlist changes to your account. Rankings and award ballots are never imported.

How do I get my Letterboxd export?

1. Open Letterboxd Settings → Data ↗
2. Click Export Your Data at the bottom of the page.
3. Upload the downloaded .zip file here. 

The ZIP never leaves this browser.
Import from IMDb

IMDb export

Preview your IMDb ratings.csv and watchlist.csv exports. 1–10 ratings are converted to the 0.5–5.0★ scale. Rankings and award ballots are never imported.

How do I get my IMDb export?

1. Open Your IMDb Ratings ↗
2. Click the three dots icon (···) or export link at the top right and choose Export.
3. (Optional) Open Your IMDb Watchlist ↗ and export your watchlist CSV.
4. Select your ratings.csv and/or watchlist.csv files here.
